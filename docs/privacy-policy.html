Privacy Policy — Polaris

Last updated: November 2026

Polaris ("the App") does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

Data stored on your device

The App stores your preferences (selected currencies, language) locally on your device. This data is not sent anywhere.

Screen Capture (Floating Bubble Feature)

If you turn on the optional floating bubble, Polaris asks Android for permission to capture the screen, and Android may show a notice while that permission is active. The app captures a single image of your screen only when you tap "Select element". The image is processed on your device with on-device text recognition to find the price, is kept in memory only while you select the price, is never saved to storage, and is never sent anywhere. Turning the bubble off ends screen capture access.

Third-party Services

Exchange rates. Polaris fetches rates from cdn.jsdelivr.net and api.frankfurter.app. Requests contain only currency codes (for example "KZT", "USD"). As with any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address.

Text recognition. Polaris uses Google ML Kit for on-device text recognition. Images and recognized text are processed on your device. According to Google, ML Kit may send technical diagnostic data to Google, such as device model and OS version, the app's package name and version, performance metrics, error codes and an installation identifier. Google states this data is encrypted in transit and not shared with third parties. Details: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure
